How to Make Classic Tiramisu

Classic tiramisu combines coffee-soaked ladyfingers with a creamy mascarpone filling and a layer of cocoa powder. This section shows the basic process of making this classic Italian dessert.

Ingredient Lists

  • Mascarpone cheese - creamy and rich base for the filling
  • Ladyfingers - light biscuits used to build the layers
  • Espresso - adds a deep coffee flavor to the dessert
  • Eggs - helps create a smooth and creamy filling
  • Sugar - adds sweetness to balance the coffee and cocoa
  • Cocoa powder - adds a slightly bitter finish on top

Steps

  1. Brew the espresso and let it cool before assembling the tiramisu.
  2. Prepare the mascarpone cream with eggs and sugar until smooth and creamy.
  3. Quickly dip each ladyfinger into the coffee without soaking them too much.
  4. Arrange the ladyfingers in a layer and spread the mascarpone cream over them.
  5. Repeat the layers until all the ingredients have been used.
  6. Chill the tiramisu and finish it with a dusting of cocoa powder before serving.
